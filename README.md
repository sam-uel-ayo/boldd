# Home

## Introduction

Boldd gives you a single set of APIs to collect payments, send money, verify identities, issue cards, and manage bills for your own business or on behalf of customers using your platform.

This page gives you a quick tour of what's available. Head to **Authentication** when you're ready to make your first request.

### Collect Payments

* **Payment Links:** A hosted checkout that aggregates multiple payment gateways behind one link, so your customers can pay with whatever method they prefer - including USD on request.
* **Direct Payments (Virtual Accounts):** Your business gets one or more dedicated NUBANs. Customers can pay directly from their bank into any of these account numbers, and every payment lands in one place - your **Boldd Wallet** regardless of which account number was used.

### Make Payments

* **Send Money (Transfers):** Send funds to any bank account in Nigeria via API or dashboard, built for financial service providers, merchants, and marketplaces. Support for additional countries (across Africa, Europe, and the US) is expanding.
* **Bill Payments:** Sell airtime, data, electricity, and Cable TV subscriptions to your users, earning a commission on every transaction, with reporting available on your dashboard.

### Cards

* **Virtual Cards:** Issue and fund virtual cards for your customers, with full lifecycle control; freeze, fund, withdraw, and terminate.
* **Physical Cards:** Issue physical debit cards, track request status, and manage activation, statements, and PINs.

### Identity & Verification

* **BVN / NIN Checks:** Verify a customer's identity against national ID records before onboarding them.
* **Liveness Check:** Confirm a real, present customer during onboarding or high-risk actions using a face-liveness challenge.
* **Customers:** Create and manage KYC-verified end users (Tier 1 or Full KYC) who transact through your platform.

### Multi-Currency & Multi-Tenancy

* **Global Accounts:** Hold and manage balances in USD, EUR, and MXN alongside your Naira wallet.
* **Sub-Accounts:** Run multiple business entities under one parent account, useful if you're a marketplace or platform onboarding your own merchants.

### Access & Limits

Most endpoints require a service grant on top of your API key — see **Getting Started → Service Access & Limits** before integrating card, transfer, or identity features.
