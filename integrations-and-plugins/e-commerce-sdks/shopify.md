# Shopify

Accept payments seamlessly on your Shopify store using Boldd's custom checkout gateway. Our Shopify integration aggregates cards, bank transfers, and local payment methods behind a single hosted checkout screen.

#### Key Features

* **Multi-Gateway Aggregation:** Allow Shopify customers to pay with their preferred local methods (cards, bank transfers, USSD).
* **Instant Verification:** Automated webhooks reconcile store orders in real-time.
* **Optimized Mobile UX:** A fast, responsive popup checkout flow designed for mobile shoppers.
